## Cross-reference collisions

Do not average all eight sources. Select one collision whose parts disagree:

- **Private impossibility × public coordinates:** combine the impossible premise
  of `0+0` with the named, observable environment logic of `Look Down`.
- **Solemn consequence × low-status carrier:** combine the emotional weight of
  `사랑하게 될 거야` with the deliberately unprestigious hook-carrier logic of
  `오이`.
- **Contradiction inventory × radical answer:** let `묘해, 너와` supply an
  unresolved chain of states and let the compression logic of `It's You` supply
  the short melodic landing.
- **Familiar symbol × failed function:** use the accessibility of `너라는 별`,
  but require the symbol to malfunction before a plain admission punctures it.

For every collision, retain one stable listener handle: a recurring rhythmic
cell, a concrete location, a one-word anchor, or a clear section return. Break
only the other axis.
