# Research basis

Use these findings as constraints and counterweights, not formulas.

## Repetition is useful until it erases novelty

- Nunes, Ordanini, and Valsesia found that lyrical repetition increases
  processing fluency and is associated with faster and broader chart adoption,
  but excessive word repetition can cancel the benefit of chorus repetition.
  Operational rule: repeat one coherent phrase; mutate its meaning through
  context instead of filling the song with the same word.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcps.2014.12.004
- A 2024 Scientific Reports study of 353,320 English-language popular-song
  lyrics found long-term decreases in lexical and structural complexity and
  increases in repetition and personal language. This is a corpus trend, not a
  quality law. Operational rule: do not equate vocabulary difficulty with
  depth.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-55742-x
- Negus and Astor identify repetition and ambiguity as integral to durable
  popular-song architecture. Operational rule: make ambiguity traceable to a
  pronoun, time, or object rather than merely withholding all context.
  https://doi.org/10.1017/S0261143015000021

## Concrete detail and prosody

- Berklee's object/destination writing method uses sensory detail to turn an
  internal feeling into an external scene. Operational rule: every section
  needs an observable action or material consequence.
  https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/songwriting-tips-try-destination-writing/
- Pat Pattison describes prosody as all elements supporting the central intent.
  Operational rule: a broken convention must intensify the song's central
  contradiction, not advertise cleverness.
  https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/prosody-in-music-and-songwriting/
- Paul Simon describes words as emerging from musical sound and rhythm.
  Operational rule: when music exists, revise text to its mouth-feel, but allow
  one load-bearing word to request a melodic revision too. This is an
  alternating constraint process, not a one-way handoff.
  https://www.paulsimon.com/en-ca/news-ca/paul-simon-discusses-his-songwriting-process/
- Research on text setting distinguishes unified, music-dominated,
  lyric-accentuated, and speech-timed relationships. Prosodic conflict can be
  expressive rather than automatically wrong.
  https://voljournals.utk.edu/gamut/vol12/iss1/3/
  https://doi.org/10.30535/mto.30.2.6
- Smith defines prosodic dissonance as conflict between spoken linguistic
  prosody and musical rendition, including stress, phrase, and intonation
  mismatches. Operational rule: establish the natural spoken baseline, make one
  mismatch intentional, inspect its surrounding context, and reject it if the
  listener cannot hear its expressive purpose.
  https://mtosmt.org/issues/mto.24.30.2/mto.24.30.2.smith.html
- Berklee frames prosody as melody, harmony, rhythm, and lyric serving a central
  intent. Operational counter-rule: total agreement is not mandatory; a
  controlled disagreement may dramatize denial, hesitation, dependence, or
  self-deception as long as the remaining elements keep the song legible.
  https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/prosody-in-music-and-songwriting/

## Korean-language implications

- Experiments reported by Yoon and Derwing indicate Korean listeners group the
  onset-plus-vowel body of a syllable differently from the English
  vowel-plus-coda rhyme unit. Operational rule: do not force English end-rhyme
  logic onto Korean; audit onset/vowel echoes, line rhythm, and grammatical
  endings.
  https://doi.org/10.1017/S0008413100001027
- Music-driven lyric generation research benefits from aligning both song-level
  semantics and sentence-level structure. Operational rule: audit global theme
  separately from line length and local phrase fit.
  https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i21.34375

## Lessons from the supplied Korean reference space

- In a Weverse Magazine interview, Hanroro describes writing personal material
  with candor, selecting words for another person, and welcoming interpretations
  not fixed to one character. Operational rule: combine emotional directness
  with an addressee aperture rather than making every line obscure.
  https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/1761
- In an interview about "Small Spring," the writer ties a shortened real-world
  season to a shortened relationship and a larger story world. Operational
  rule: a metaphor becomes memorable when its physical mechanism and narrative
  mechanism are the same.
  https://isplus.com/article/view/isp202504160132
